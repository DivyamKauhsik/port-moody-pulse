2026 General Election

How to vote in Port Moody.

Election day is Saturday, October 17. Everything you need — nothing you don’t — verified against the City’s official voting information.

Three ways to vote

  • 1
    Vote earlyWednesday, October 7 or Saturday, October 10, 8am–8pm. Wellness Room, Port Moody Recreation Complex (300 Ioco Road).
  • 2
    Vote by mailApply by 12pm on Friday, October 9 at portmoody.ca/vote.
  • 3
    Vote on election daySaturday, October 17, 8am–8pm, at any of the four locations below.

Where to vote on October 17

  • Port Moody Recreation Complex — Gymnasium, 300 Ioco Road
  • Kyle Centre — 125 Kyle Street
  • Heritage Mountain Community Centre — 200 Panorama Place
  • Glenayre Community Centre — 492 Glencoe Drive

Your ballot

Pick up to 1 mayor, 6 councillors, and 2 school trustees. Leave some blank if you like — your ballot still counts, and you don’t need to vote in every category.

Can you vote?

You need to be 18 or older on October 17, a Canadian citizen, and a BC resident for at least 6 months. Not registered? You can register right at the voting place — just bring two pieces of ID, one with your signature (driver’s licence, BC Services Card, passport, utility bill, and others all work).

Good to know. Phones and cameras stay off and put away inside voting places. And last time around, only 36% of Port Moody voted — 8,989 ballots decided the entire council. Yours counts more than you think.

Full details, including eligibility for non-resident property owners: portmoody.ca/vote.
