About Port Moody Pulse

The person behind
Port Moody Pulse

A neighbour who pays attention, verifies what he publishes, and genuinely cares about this city.

Hi, I’m Divyam.

I’ve lived in Port Moody for six years, in Suter Brook. I moved here as an immigrant, didn’t know anyone, and slowly fell in love with this city the way a lot of people do: one walk along the Shoreline Trail, one Friday night on Murray Street, one conversation with a neighbour at a time.

Port Moody Pulse started because I kept missing things.

A grocery store breaking ground down the street. A neighbourhood fundraiser. A 43-year volunteer doing something remarkable a few blocks away. The information existed — it just wasn’t in one place, and it wasn’t written for the people who actually live here.

So I started writing it myself.

I’m not a journalist by training. I’m a neighbour who pays attention, verifies what I publish, and genuinely cares about this city. I also happen to have spent 15 years working in digital strategy and change management — which mostly just means I know how to find things out and how to communicate clearly.

Outside the newsletter, I’m a portrait artist. I’ve been painting for twelve years — it’s the other way I try to pay attention to people. You can find that work at @divyamkaushik_art on Instagram.

Every week I read the council minutes, talk to local business owners, check the city’s construction updates, and pull together the things worth knowing into one Friday morning email. Free, no ads, no agenda.

Port Moody Pulse is independent. It’s not affiliated with the City of Port Moody, any political candidate, or any business. When a business or organization supports the newsletter, I say so — every time.

Divyam Kaushik
Founder, Port Moody Pulse
