The Man Behind the Boathouse

Gordon Clay didn’t set out to build a boathouse. In the late 1990s, he was simply one of the Port Moody residents who believed the city’s rowers, sailors and paddlers deserved a permanent place to call home — and he spent the next several years making sure it happened.

A founding member of the Old Mill Boathouse Society, Gordon was there for the sod-turning in 2001 — and when the $1.1 million waterfront facility opened on July 1, 2002, his fingerprints were all over it. Earlier this month, the community recognized his contribution at the Boathouse.
