BC Provincial Election 2026

Meet your candidates.

Four candidates are running for MLA in Port Moody-Burquitlam, and final voting day is Saturday, October 24. Each card below carries the candidate’s photo, party, and bio facts drawn from official sources. The riding covers the entire City of Port Moody, plus Anmore, Belcarra, and part of Coquitlam. Port Moody Pulse doesn’t endorse candidates; this guide is a voter service from your neighbours.

Facts from official sources means each card’s facts are drawn from the candidate’s official biography or campaign page. Cards were not reviewed by the candidates.

Oct 16–21 — Advance voting (8am–8pm)
Oct 18 — Mail ballot request deadline (today)
Oct 24 — Final voting day (8am–8pm)
Don’t miss the provincial result. Final voting day is Sat Oct 24 — get the Port Moody-Burquitlam result and what it means for our city in the free Friday newsletter.

i.

Where to vote

Advance voting runs Oct 16–21, 8am–8pm, and you can vote at any advance voting place in BC. Near Port Moody:

  • Klahanie Canoe Club, 501 Klahanie Dr, Port Moody — Oct 16–17 and Oct 19–21 (closed Sun Oct 18)
  • Coquitlam Presbyterian Church, 948 Como Lake Ave, Coquitlam — Oct 16–21

On final voting day (Sat Oct 24, 8am–8pm) vote at your assigned place. Port Moody locations include Aspenwood Elementary, Heritage Woods Secondary, Klahanie Canoe Club, Mountain Meadows Elementary, Seaview Community School, and École Moody Middle.

ii.

What ID to bring

Bring one piece of photo ID (BC driver’s licence, BCID, or photo BC Services Card), or two documents with your name (at least one showing your current address), or have another voter vouch for you. Full list at elections.bc.ca.

iii.

No endorsements

Candidates appear alphabetically by surname: Chou, Glumac, McEwen, Rawji. Facts come from official sources, and nothing here is a recommendation. Port Moody Pulse does not endorse candidates.

Wei Chou

Running for MLA, Port Moody-Burquitlam
BC Green Party
Facts from official sources
About
  • Entrepreneur, supply chain professional and community organizer; came to Canada in 1997 and grew up in Port Moody. [bcgreens.ca]
  • Founded a Vancouver tech startup in 2019, growing it to $1.7M revenue with a 14-person team; wound it down ensuring employees were paid. [bcgreens.ca]
  • Mentors entrepreneurs and researchers through Innovation UBC, Lab2Market and New Ventures BC; focus on social enterprise and clean tech. [bcgreens.ca]
  • During the COVID-19 PPE shortage helped coordinate sourcing and delivery of 20,000 surgical masks, N95 respirators and hospital gowns for frontline healthcare workers. [bcgreens.ca]

Rick Glumac

Running for MLA, Port Moody-Burquitlam
BC NDP
Facts from official sources
About
  • First elected MLA for Port Moody-Coquitlam (renamed Port Moody-Burquitlam for 2024) in 2017; re-elected 2020 and 2024. [news.gov.bc.ca]
  • Appointed Minister of State for Artificial Intelligence and New Technologies in July 2025; previously Minister of State for Trade, Parliamentary Secretary for Technology, and Premier’s Liaison to Washington state and the Pacific Northwest. [news.gov.bc.ca]
  • SFU Bachelor of Applied Science in Electronics Engineering; before politics worked in tech including animation on Shrek 2 and Madagascar, video games (FIFA Soccer), and mobile apps for Fortune 500 companies. [rickglumac.mla.bcndpcaucus.ca]
  • Port Moody city councillor 2011–2017; chaired the city’s Economic Development, Environmental Protection, Heritage, Arts and Culture, and Community Care committees. [rickglumac.mla.bcndpcaucus.ca]
