Culture Days — through Oct 4

City-wide · Free · All ages

A national celebration of arts and culture, with a Port Moody hub hosted by the City: public art tours, talks, exhibitions and hands-on workshops, together with the Port Moody Public Library and PoMoArts. Get behind the scenes with local artists and creators.

Blanket Exercise

Wed, Sep 30 · National Day for Truth and Reconciliation

Offered in recognition of the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation. The Blanket Exercise honours residential school Survivors, their families and communities, and the children who never returned home — a chance to deepen understanding and reflect on our shared history.

Remembrance Day Ceremony

Wed, Nov 11 · Port Moody Legion to the cenotaph

The Port Moody Legion honours veterans and their families. Hundreds of cadets, Scouts, Guides and first responders join the parade to the cenotaph for the official act of remembrance, the two-minute silence and the laying of the wreaths.
