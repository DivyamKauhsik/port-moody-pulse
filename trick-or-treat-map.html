Halloween 2026

The Trick-or-Treat Map

Every Halloween, Port Moody goes all out. This year we’re mapping it — the spookiest decorated houses in town, plus the Halloween events happening around town, plotted for Halloween night. Tap any pin for the details. Built from neighbour sightings, one tag at a time.

1. Spot
See a decked-out house on your walk? Snap it.
2. Tag + follow
Tag @portmoodypulse in your Story or post, add the street name in your caption (just the street, never the house number), and follow @portmoodypulse and @cannolikingvancouver. Every tagged house is an entry in the Cannoli King draw: three cannoli flights, drawn Sun, Nov 1. Winners collect their prize from Cannoli King, 950-1125 Seaborne Ave, Port Coquitlam, by Wed, Nov 4 at 5 PM.
3. We pin it
We use the street you name to drop a pin on that block, never at an exact address, and we update the map all week as photos come in. The best sightings get reposted to our Stories.
The first pins drop soon. We’re collecting sightings now — spot a spooky house? Tag @portmoodypulse on Instagram and it lands here.
